### Neighborhood Definitions
- **Parent / Super-domain**: Comparative Performance Analytics (measuring marginal difference between treatment and control states).
- **Siblings**: Raw Accuracy Metrics, Precision/Recall/F1, Latency P95/P99 Profiling, Pure Operational Cost Modeling.
- **Children / Sub-concepts**: Baseline Subtraction, Net Quality Lift ($\Delta$), Token Overhead Accounting, Cost-Adjusted Efficiency Ratio.
- **Adjacent / Cross-over**: Randomized Controlled Trials (A/B Testing), Uplift / Prescriptive Modeling, Causal Inference & Average Treatment Effect (ATE), Microeconomics (Marginal Utility).
- **Frontier / Emerging**: Multi-Objective Pareto Lift Frontiers, Counterfactual Trajectory Evaluation, Automated Marginal Utility Skill Pruning.
