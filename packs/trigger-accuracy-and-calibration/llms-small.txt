# Trigger Accuracy & Calibration: Compact Reference

## Quick Rules
1. **Corpus Size**: 20 queries total (10 positive, 10 near-miss negative).
2. **Positive Distribution**:
   - 2-3 direct ("format this SQL")
   - 2-3 symptom ("query is timing out on join")
   - 2-3 terse/informal ("fix sql plz")
   - 2-3 context-rich (schema details, file paths)
3. **Negative Distribution**:
   - 5-6 near-misses (adjacent tools/competitor skills)
   - 2-3 base model tasks (generic syntax/explaining code)
   - 2 explicit non-triggers (covered by SKIP rules)
4. **Acceptance Thresholds**:
   - Positive Activation Rate: $\ge 90\%$ (min 9/10)
   - False Positive Rate: $\le 10\%$ (max 1/10)
   - Sampling: 3 runs per query; trigger if mean $\ge 0.5$
5. **Data Split**: 60% train (12 queries), 40% held-out validation (8 queries).
6. **Constraint**: `description` field max 1024 characters.
