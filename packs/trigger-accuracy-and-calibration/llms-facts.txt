# Trigger Accuracy and Calibration: Atomic Facts

- [Standard trigger evaluation corpus consists of 20 queries divided into 10 positive intents and 10 near-miss negatives.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/optimizing-descriptions.md)
- [Target activation rate for in-scope positive triggers must be at or above 90 percent.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/optimizing-descriptions.md)
- [False positive trigger rate on negative near-miss queries must not exceed 10 percent.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/optimizing-descriptions.md)
- [Agent skill descriptions are bounded to a maximum length of 1024 characters to prevent routing context pollution.](https://agentskills.io/specification.md)
- [Stochastic routing variation requires executing each evaluation prompt across 3 runs to calculate expected activation frequency.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/optimizing-descriptions.md)
- [Positive test suites must include direct, symptom-based, terse, and context-rich queries.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/optimizing-descriptions.md)
- [Negative suites must incorporate hard near-misses that share terminology with competing skills.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/optimizing-descriptions.md)
- [Evaluation suites must partition queries into a 60 percent training set and a 40 percent held-out validation set to avoid overfitting.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/optimizing-descriptions.md)
