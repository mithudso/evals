# Skill Evals: Atomic Facts

- [Standard trigger evaluation corpus consists of approximately 20 queries, divided into 10 positive intents and 10 near-miss negatives.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/optimizing-descriptions.md)
- [Target performance threshold for skill descriptions requires positive activation >= 90% and false positive rate <= 10%.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/optimizing-descriptions.md)
- [Skills use progressive disclosure where only name and description are loaded into the always-on routing context.](https://agentskills.io/specification.md)
- [Agent skill descriptions are subject to a strict character ceiling of 1024 characters to prevent context window bloat.](https://agentskills.io/specification.md)
- [Functional evals compare performance across isolated with_skill and without_skill workspace directories.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/evaluating-skills.md)
- [Multi-run trigger accuracy benchmarks execute each query 3 times to derive a statistical activation rate.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/optimizing-descriptions.md)
- [Objective assertions verify explicit output structures, schemas, and content rather than subjective vibes.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/evaluating-skills.md)
- [Standard deviation across eval iterations detects flaky assertions caused by ambiguous instructions or high variance.](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/evaluating-skills.md)
